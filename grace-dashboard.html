Understanding the Risk Score

▼

The Risk Score is calculated by counting the number of chronic disease ICD codes documented for each patient. These include:

  • Diabetes (E10, E11): Type 1 and Type 2 diabetes
  • Substance Use Disorder (F10-F19): Alcohol and drug use disorders
  • Pain Conditions (G89): Chronic pain syndromes
  • Personality Disorders (F60): Various personality disorder diagnoses
  • Hypertension (I10): High blood pressure
  • Heart Disease (I50): Heart failure and related conditions
  • COPD (J44): Chronic obstructive pulmonary disease
  • Depression (F32-F33): Depressive disorders
  • Asthma (J45): Asthma and reactive airway disease

Risk Score Range: 0 (no conditions) to 5+ (five or more chronic conditions)
